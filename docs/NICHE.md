# First niche (decided 5 Oct 2026)

**Meaning-first Adinkra and African-heritage designs.** Each design pairs one Adinkra symbol or Akan proverb with its meaning, in a modern style (clean lettering, minimalist line or geometric art). Buyers: diaspora adults and gift buyers in the US and UK, plus home-décor and culture-conscious shoppers.

Products (up to 3): **poster, mug, tee.** Drop any whose real margin falls under the floor (`MARGIN_FLOOR_PCT`).

Seasonal layer to add later: Black History Month (Feb), Juneteenth, Kwanzaa.

## Rules for the Research Lab and Factory
- Symbol names, meanings and proverb spellings must be checked against a reference list before use. A wrong proverb is a quality failure.
- The quality gate compares generated symbols to reference images; reject distorted or invented symbols.
- Respectful treatment: no clip-art mash-ups, no trivialising sacred or royal symbols.
- No real people, organisations, brands or lyrics.
- Ask a local adviser whether commercial use of traditional Ghanaian cultural expressions needs permission or acknowledgement (open item).

## Open checks
- Demand validation by the Research Lab (search and buyer signals) before the first batch.
- Real margin per product from Printify blueprints.
- Timing: Black Friday/Kwanzaa small push; Black History Month as main launch (designs ready in ~8 weeks).
